# NNNN. Title that states the decision

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decisions: the ids from the re-architecture plan this record covers, or `none (made during the build)`

## Context

What forced a decision: the problem, the constraints, and what was tried or measured. Two or three short
paragraphs. Link to the plan or research note that holds the detail.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so that someone can tell whether a change follows it or breaks it.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: what becomes easier, what becomes harder, what is ruled out, and what has to be
watched.
